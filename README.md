# Insurance Operations and Business Records Toolkit

Simple working templates for policy follow-up, claims documentation, field observations and materials records. These areas reflect my insurance experience and earlier work with construction materials records.

The files are starting structures for a real assignment. They contain no customer cases, claim decisions, company stock figures or invented achievements.

## Choose the template you need

| Task | File | What it helps organise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New business and renewals | [Policy follow-up register](templates/policy_followup_register.csv) | Source references, outstanding information and the next action. |
| Claims document follow-up | [Claims document tracker](templates/claims_document_tracker.csv) | Documents requested, received items and questions still open. |
| Field observations | [Field observation register](templates/field_observation_register.csv) | What was observed, its source and the information that needs verification. |
| Store and materials records | [Materials issue register](templates/materials_issue_register.csv) | Item descriptions, units, quantities issued and the supporting reference. |
| A short client or team update | [Handover note](docs/handover_note_template.md) | Completed work, unresolved questions and agreed next steps. |

## How I would use these files

I would first agree what the client needs to track and the source documents available. I would adapt the columns to that workflow, keep original information separate from confirmed corrections, and give each outstanding question an action and a responsible person.

The policy register can support work involving motor, life, health, fire, cargo or livestock cover. Requirements differ by product and insurer. Use the actual quotation, policy wording and claims instructions rather than assuming that one checklist fits every product.

For field data, distinguish an observation from a statement made by someone else. An observed housing issue, for example, does not by itself establish the cause of a livestock loss. Technical or veterinary findings should be attributed to the qualified person and their report.

For materials records, keep the unit beside the quantity. A number of bags and a weight in kilograms are different measures. Record stock receipts, opening balances and physical counts separately if the task also needs a full stock reconciliation.

## Practical delivery

The CSV templates can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets. They have column headings only. Keep completed files in the client's agreed private working location, not this public repository. A handover should identify the files supplied, their date and the specific questions still requiring confirmation.
